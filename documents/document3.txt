# FINANCIAL AND LOAN APPROVAL POLICIES

## 1. Policy Overview

ABC Financial Services Pvt Ltd follows internal financial policies to evaluate loan applications and manage credit risk.

These policies are fictional and intended for software testing.

## 2. Loan Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the following requirements:

- Minimum Age: 21 years.
- Maximum Age: 60 years at loan application.
- Minimum Monthly Income: INR 25,000.
- Minimum Employment Experience: 1 year.
- Valid identity and address proof are required.

## 3. Credit Score Requirements

The company uses credit scores to assess borrower creditworthiness.

Credit Score Above 750:
Eligible for standard loan consideration.

Credit Score Between 650 and 750:
Requires additional financial review.

Credit Score Below 650:
Requires enhanced risk assessment and may be rejected.

A credit score alone does not determine final loan approval.

## 4. Required Documents

Applicants must submit:

1. Aadhaar Card or other accepted identity proof.
2. PAN Card.
3. Latest three months' salary slips.
4. Latest six months' bank statements.
5. Address proof.
6. Employment verification documents.

## 5. Loan Approval Process

The loan approval process contains five stages:

Stage 1: Application Submission.

Stage 2: Document Verification.

Stage 3: Income and Employment Verification.

Stage 4: Credit Risk Assessment.

Stage 5: Final Loan Approval or Rejection.

## 6. Debt-to-Income Ratio

The company evaluates the applicant's monthly debt obligations compared with monthly income.

Preferred Debt-to-Income Ratio: Below 40%.

If the ratio exceeds 40%, the application requires additional financial assessment.

## 7. Risk Assessment

The risk management team evaluates:

- Credit history.
- Monthly income.
- Existing financial obligations.
- Employment stability.
- Previous repayment history.

## 8. Data Security Policy

Customer financial information must be protected.

Employees must:

- Use authorized systems to access customer information.
- Never share customer financial information with unauthorized individuals.
- Follow access control policies.
- Protect sensitive personal information.
- Report suspected data breaches.

## 9. Loan Rejection Conditions

Applications may be rejected when:

- Required documents are missing.
- Income information cannot be verified.
- Credit risk is considered too high.
- Applicant eligibility requirements are not met.

## 10. Final Decision

The loan approval team makes the final decision after reviewing the applicant's documents, financial details, and credit risk assessment.

Note: This is a fictional financial policy document for RAG development and testing.
